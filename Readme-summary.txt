================================================================================
WAGADU ANTIQUITY AGE - ABILITIES SUMMARY
================================================================================

CIVILIZATION ABILITY: Gassire's Lute
────────────────────────────────────
  • +2 Influence for each Trade Route
  • +30% Production towards constructing the Larabanga Mosque


UNIQUE UNIT
────────────────────────────────────
Trans-Saharan Trader
  • Wagadu Unique Merchant unit
  • Ignores movement penalties from Vegetated Terrain


UNIQUE BUILDINGS
────────────────────────────────────
Dome Ka Yoroba (Influence Building - Ageless)
  • +1 Influence
  • +1 Influence Adjacency for Quarters, Resources, and Wonders

Yiri Senuman (Happiness Building - Ageless)
  • +3 Happiness
  • +1 Happiness Adjacency for Quarters, Mountains, Natural Wonders, and Wonders


UNIQUE DISTRICT
────────────────────────────────────
Ouagadou (place of the Wague)
  • Created by constructing both Dome Ka Yoroba and Yiri Senuman in the same District
  • +2 Resource Capacity in that city
  • +1 Gold on trade routes from this city


UNIQUE WONDER
────────────────────────────────────
Larabanga Mosque
  • Has a Great Work slot
  • Generates a free Codex
  • Additional Happiness for each Great Work in the Settlement


CIVICS & TRADITIONS
────────────────────────────────────
Bambouk Civic
  • Gain a free Trans-Saharan Trader unit when establishing a trade route

Dinar Civic
  • +2 Happiness in source city for each Trade Route
  • +1 Food in source city for each Trade Route

Ghana Civic
  • Increased Land Trade Route Range per Suzerain
  • Increased Sea Trade Route Range per Suzerain

Dinga I-III Traditions
  • Dinga I: Additional Influence on the Palace for each adjacent Quarter
  • Dinga II: Additional Influence on Palace and Town Halls in Distant Lands for each adjacent Quarter
  • Dinga III: Additional Influence on Palace and Town Halls for each adjacent Quarter


================================================================================
PLAYSTYLE FOCUS: Trade Routes & Diplomacy
────────────────────────────────────────────
The Wagadu civilization specializes in Trade Routes and Diplomatic Influence,
with bonuses that reward establishing trade connections, gaining happiness through
commerce, and extending trade route range through Suzerain relationships. The 
unique units and buildings enhance these strengths through production bonuses and
adjacency mechanics.
